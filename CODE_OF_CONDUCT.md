## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, professional, and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to **GeoVigilant Argus Eye**.

Participants are expected to treat all community members with respect, professionalism, and integrity across all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and documentation discussions.

---

## Expected Behavior

* **Constructive Collaboration**: Provide clear, actionable feedback and mentor new contributors.
* **Professional Civility**: Maintain respectful discourse across all communications.
* **Community & Operational Focus**: Prioritize the stability, privacy, security, and performance of open geospatial intelligence.
* **Inclusive Communication**: Use welcoming, professional language and respect diverse viewpoints.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or exclusionary behavior.
* Publishing private credentials, API keys, internal IP addresses, or sensitive personal data.
* Introducing malicious code, intentional backdoors, spam, or abusive network requests.
* Unauthorized access attempts against community infrastructure.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately via GitHub Security Advisories or maintainer contact channels.

Thank you for helping keep **GeoVigilant Argus Eye** a collaborative, secure, and productive open-source intelligence community! 🌍🛰️
